## Overview

An end-to-end system that designs, back-tests and runs trading strategies automatically.

## Strategies

| Strategy | Signals |
| --- | --- |
| Portfolio Rebalancing | Periodic rebalancing on performance |
| Resistance Breakout | Price/volume breakouts |
| Renko + OBV | Renko bricks with On-Balance Volume |
| Renko + MACD | Renko bricks with MACD crossovers |

Indicators used across strategies include **MACD**, **RSI** and **Bollinger Bands**.

## Evaluation

Each strategy was back-tested and compared on **CAGR**, **Sharpe ratio** and **maximum drawdown**.

## Deployment

The system runs on **AWS EC2**, with **cron** scheduling for real-time execution through **MetaTrader**. Market data comes from **yfinance** and the **Alpha Vantage API**.
