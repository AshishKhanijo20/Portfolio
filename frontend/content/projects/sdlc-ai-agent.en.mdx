## The problem

Much of the software development lifecycle is repetitive coordination work — exactly the kind of task an AI agent could take over, *if* it can safely reach the right tools.

## Approach

- Built an **AI agent prototype** that automates tasks across the SDLC.
- Connected it to internal systems through **custom tools** and **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** integrations.
- Worked with **security, DevOps and developer teams** on secure coding, threat modeling and safe deployment of AI systems.
- Shared results through **workshops and demos** to help teams adopt the tooling.

## What I learned

Building an agent is the easy part — making it **safe, observable and trusted** by the teams who use it is where most of the engineering goes.
